# Source Documents for the Manuscript

This folder contains the specific versions of the NCCN and ESMO guidelines used for the analysis in our manuscript, "Benchmarking LLMs and Prompt Engineering Strategies for Consensus and Frontier Knowledge in Microsatellite Instability Cancers".

These documents are permanently archived here to ensure the reproducibility of our research, as these clinical practice guidelines are updated frequently.

All guidelines are copyrighted materials of their respective organizations (NCCN, ESMO).  For the most current versions and for any other use, please visit their official websites.
